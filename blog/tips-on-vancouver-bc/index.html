Tips on Vancouver BC

Aug 23, 2025

Things on this page are fragmentary and immature notes/thoughts of the author. Please read with your own judgement!

Credit Card

Use Google Wallet Pay and/or Apple Pay if possible. At least, you should use credit cards with good fraud protection (e.g., Chase).

EV Charging

The Tesla Supercharger at "4660 Garden City Rd, Richmond" is a good one.

  1. 40 chargers
  2. V3 superchargers and thus open to third-party EVs
  3. good charging power (250kw)
  4. food & shopping nearby

Vancouver Aquarium

There are 2 level-2 chargers in front of the entrance to Vancouver Aquarium, which is a huge advantage for EVs as parking spots can be hard to find.

Stanley Park

  1. Paid parking session is valid at any parking lot in Stanley Park.

Restaurants in Vancourver

  • Noodle Arts 满福兰州拉面（粥粉面）- Vancouver, near Stanley Park

  • 花馬春天雲南過橋米線 Flower & Horse in Spring - Vancouver, near Stanley Park

Road Trip from WA to Vancourver BC

  1. VanderYacht Park is a good park to stop for a lunch picnic.

US/Canada Customers and Border Protection

  1. Border Crossings

  2. Nexus

  3. Duty Free stores which allows you cut in the waiting queue with purchases.

    • West Coast Tax & Duty Free

    • Duty Free Americas

    • Peace Arch Duty Free
