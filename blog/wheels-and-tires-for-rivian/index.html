Wheels and Tires for Rivian

Sep 30, 2025

Things on this page are fragmentary and immature notes/thoughts of the author. Please read with your own judgement!

Size of Wheels

The discussion About to order R1S - struggling with 22" vs 20" with snow in mind? on Reddit strongly suggest 20-inch wheels against 22-inch wheels.

Feature 20-inch Wheels 22-inch Wheels
Ride Comfort High (Thicker sidewall absorbs impacts) Moderate to Low (Thinner sidewall, harsher)
Off-Road Capability Excellent (Better articulation, durability) Poor (High damage risk, less tire deflection)
Aesthetics Functional, Rugged Sporty, Premium, Aggressive
Handling (On-Road) Good (Slightly less precise) Excellent (More direct and responsive)
Durability (Wheels) High (More protection from impacts) Low (More vulnerable to damage)
Tire Cost Generally Lower Generally Higher
Damage Risk Lower (Less prone to pinch flats, rim damage) Higher (More susceptible to damage)
Snow/Ice Performance Generally Better Generally Worse
Efficiency (Range) Potentially marginally better Potentially marginally worse
Wheel Aftermarket Avail. High Low
Tire Aftermarket Avail. High Low
